Quick questions, answered

Stuff people ask before they call.

How fast can you come out? +

Most lovebug calls get scheduled same-day or next-day. The acid in lovebug remains starts eating clear coat within 48 hours of baking on hot paint — the sooner the better.

Will the bugs damage my paint permanently? +

They will if you leave them. Lovebug guts are acidic. On a hot Florida hood they can etch the clear coat in two or three days, leaving permanent dull spots even after wash. We use a pH-neutral pre-soak that lifts the residue without grinding it into the finish.

Do you come to me? +

Yes. I show up to your driveway or workplace and work with whatever your spot has — your hose, your power, your shade. We figure it out together. Service area: all of Pasco County (Land O' Lakes, Wesley Chapel, Trinity, New Port Richey, Zephyrhills) plus North Hillsborough (Lutz, Carrollwood, north Tampa).

What if it rains? +

We reschedule at no charge. Florida rain is part of the deal — we'd rather move the slot than half-finish a job.
